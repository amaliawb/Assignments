1. Upload your data

Three required files plus two optional ones (a reference atlas and a single-cell imaging export). The script's comments describe each format. FACS and scRNA-seq do not have to come from the same samples: the sample sheet says, for every sample, which method measured it (column {{ analysis.METHOD_COLUMN }}: FACS or scRNA-seq). A pool of urine from several people is one FACS sample.

The reference atlas is a single-cell dataset of urine with known cell types. With it, section E compares your FACS and Flex results with the atlas. Without it, section E is skipped.

One row per FACS cell with its population and S8 imaging features (exported from FlowJo). With it, section F adds cell-level plots: distributions, % outlier cells per sample and a morphology map.

No data yet?

Make a made-up demo dataset and analyse that instead: scRNA-seq of 12 samples from 8 people (4 Healthy, 4 Disease) and FACS of other people (one pool of healthy urine and 6 Disease patients), plus a reference atlas of 8 healthy donors and single-cell imaging data.
